# Account Scoring Methodology

This project uses a 100-point framework to prioritize B2B target accounts based on ICP fit, buying signals, and sales accessibility.

## 1. ICP Fit — 50 Points

### Retailer Complexity — 15 points
- 1 major retailer: 3
- 2 major retailers: 6
- 3 major retailers: 10
- 4+ major retailers: 15

### Product / Brand Complexity — 15 points
- Low: 3
- Medium: 8
- High: 15

### Company Scale — 10 points
- Mid-Market: 4
- Upper Mid-Market: 7
- Enterprise: 10

### Use-Case Alignment — 10 points
- Weak: 2
- Moderate: 6
- Strong: 10

## 2. Buying Signals — 30 Points

- Recent ecommerce or retail initiative: 10
- AI or data-transformation initiative: 8
- Relevant ecommerce / digital hiring: 6
- Expansion or growth trigger: 6

## 3. Sales Accessibility — 20 Points

- Relevant primary buyer identified: 8
- Secondary stakeholder identified: 5
- Strong personalization trigger: 4
- High-quality public evidence: 3

## Priority Tiers

- 85–100: Tier 1A
- 75–84: Tier 1B
- 60–74: Tier 2
- Below 60: Tier 3

## Evidence Rules

Buying signals and account-specific claims must be supported by publicly available evidence.

Unverified information will not receive scoring credit.

Facts, inferences, and sales hypotheses will be clearly distinguished.

This is an independent portfolio case study and does not use confidential or proprietary company data.
